Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MONI Interiors respects your privacy. This page explains what information we receive when you contact us and how we use it.

Information you share

When you use the consultation form on this website, your name, phone number, project location, required service and project details are used only to prepare a WhatsApp message on your own device. The website does not store or send this information to us. We receive it only if you choose to send the message in WhatsApp.

If you call, WhatsApp or visit us, we may receive your name, phone number and details about your interior project.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your requirements.
  • To prepare quotations, plan measurements and arrange installation.
  • To share warranty and after-sales information about products you purchase.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to deliver your project (for example, with product suppliers) or where required by law.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash, and WhatsApp conversations are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.
