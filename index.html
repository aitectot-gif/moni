Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help you plan your interior project.

What services does MONI Interiors offer?

MONI Interiors offers UPVC and PVC interior solutions such as modular kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, cupboards, doors, dressing tables, TV units, showcases, pooja units, wall panels, ceiling panels and partitions, subject to current product availability.

Which locations do you serve?

We serve Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Kerala. Contact the team to confirm service availability and project arrangements for your specific location.

What warranty is available?

Our products come with up to a 15-year material warranty and a 5-year hardware warranty. Coverage, eligibility, exclusions and applicable products are confirmed in the written warranty terms shared with your quotation.

Are UPVC and PVC products waterproof?

Many products offer water-resistant properties, but performance depends on the material, fittings, joints, installation and intended application. Ask the team to confirm suitability for your space.

How can I request a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp 73394 16967 with your location, the service you need and any room measurements or reference photographs you have available.
